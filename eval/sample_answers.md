## 1. What is IBS, and what is its main purpose for a new business owner?

*groq:qwen/qwen3.8-27b · 15.2s · 2 citation(s) · check: 0 removed, 3 re-cited*

**Short answer**: The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the statutory body that grants licences for product certification under the BIS Act, 2016, and the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 [1]. For a new business owner, its main purpose is to assess the manufacturer’s infrastructure, production process, quality control, and testing capabilities to ensure the product conforms to the relevant Indian Standard before granting a licence [1][2].

**Details**:
*   **Role of BIS**: BIS grants a licence based on the successful assessment of the manufacturing infrastructure, production process, quality control, and testing capabilities/arrangements of the manufacturer through a visit to its manufacturing premises [1].
*   **Conformity Assessment**: Conformity of the product to the relevant standard(s) is established through third-party laboratory testing, testing during the visit to the premises, or a combination of both [1].
*   **Compulsory Certification**: If a product is listed under compulsory certification by the Central Government as per the BIS Act, 2016, it is compulsory for the manufacturer to obtain a BIS licence [2].
*   **Essential Requirements**: To obtain a licence, the manufacturer must have requisite manufacturing infrastructure, appropriate process controls, quality control, and testing capabilities for the product as per the relevant Indian Standard (ISS), and the product must conform to all requirements laid down in the ISS [2].

## 2. What documents are required to complete the IBS registration process?

*groq:qwen/qwen3.8-27b · 21.7s · 4 citation(s) · check: 2 removed, 13 re-cited*

**Short answer**: The BIS Product Certification (Scheme-I) application requires a filled application form, proof of applicant status (e.g., small scale registration), details of the Indian Standard and product varieties, test reports from BIS or recognized laboratories, evidence of manufacturing infrastructure and quality control personnel, and payment of the application fee.

**Details**:
*   **Application Form**: Must be completely filled with the name and address of the manufacturing unit matching the attached government documents [1].
*   **Applicant Status**: Indication of whether the unit is large or small scale; if small scale, a valid Registration Certificate from Local/State authority or a Chartered Accountant must be attached [1].
*   **Product Details**: The applicable Indian Standard (IS) number with the correct title, and the designation, grade, type, variety, or size of the product must be mentioned [1].
*   **Test Reports**: Test reports must cover all requirements of the Indian Standard and show passing results [2]. For the "simplified procedure," the test report must be in original, from BIS or its recognized laboratories, not older than one month from the date of submission, and cover all standard requirements [2].
*   **Manufacturing Infrastructure**: Evidence that the applicant possesses requisite manufacturing infrastructure, installed capacity, and all necessary machinery as per the manufacturing process [3]. If processes are outsourced, valid agreements and control mechanisms must be provided [1].
*   **Quality Control**: Documentary evidence that qualified and experienced Quality Control (QC) personnel are employed on a permanent basis, including copies of qualification certificates and appointment letters [2].
*   **Raw Materials**: Test reports or suppliers’ test certificates showing that raw materials/components meet the standard's requirements [2].
*   **Plant Layout**: A copy of the plant layout clearly indicating locations of machinery, laboratory, office, workshop, amenities, and storage areas [2].
*   **History**: Information regarding any earlier BIS licences held or cancelled, and any currently held BIS licences [1].
*   **Signature**: The application must be signed by the CEO or an authorized signatory with attached authorization [1].
*   **Undertaking**: For applications under the simplified procedure, an undertaking on the applicant’s letter-head regarding corrective actions if verification samples fail [2].

**Step-by-step**:
1. Fill out the application form completely, ensuring the name and address match the attached government documents [1].
2. Attach the Registration Certificate if the applicant is a small scale unit [1].
3. Specify the Indian Standard number, title, and product varieties/grades [1].
4. Submit test reports from BIS or recognized laboratories that are not older than one month (for simplified procedure) and cover all standard requirements [2].
6. Submit documents proving the employment of qualified QC personnel and compliance of raw materials [2].
7. Include information on previous or current BIS licences [1].
8. Sign the application by the CEO or authorized signatory [1].
9. Pay the requisite application fee of Rs. 1000/- [4].

**Documents, fees and timelines**:
*   **Application Fee**: Rs. 1000/- (plus taxes for SAARC country applicants) [4].
*   **Test Report Validity**: For the simplified procedure, the test report must not be older than one month from the date of submission [2].
*   **Inspection Fee**: Rs.

**What happens if...**:
*   If the application is under the simplified procedure and verification samples fail, the licence will be put under Stop Marking by BIS until corrective actions are taken and fresh samples pass independent testing [2].
*   Violation of licence conditions may result in cancellation of the licence as per Regulation 11 of BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 [3].

## 3. What are the main steps a new business owner needs to follow to get started with BIS?

*gemini:gemini-3.5-flash-lite · 11.5s · 2 citation(s) · check: 0 removed, 2 re-cited*

**Short answer**: A new business owner must submit an application online in Form-V along with relevant documents and a self-evaluation cum verification report [1]. Separate applications are required for each product, Indian Standard, and factory location [2].

**Details**:
The Bureau grants a licence based on a successful assessment of the manufacturing infrastructure, production process, quality control, and testing capabilities through a visit to the manufacturing premises [1]. Conformity of the product to the relevant standard is established through third-party laboratory testing, factory testing, or a combination of both [1].

**Step-by-step**:
1. Search for the appropriate Indian Standard corresponding to your product on the Manak Online website [2].
2. Submit an application online in Form-V as specified in the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations 2018 (Scheme-I) along with relevant documents and a self-evaluation cum verification report (Annexure-I) [1].
3. Undergo assessment where BIS evaluates your manufacturing infrastructure, production process, quality control, and testing capabilities via a visit to your premises [1].
4. Provide samples for testing either through third-party laboratory testing or factory testing depending on the applicable product category and option [1].

## 4. What is the penalty for using the ISI mark without a licence?

*gemini:gemini-3.5-flash-lite · 8.9s · 1 citation(s) · check: 0 removed, 0 re-cited*

**Short answer**:
Using the Standard Mark (ISI mark) without a licence is a contravention of section 17, and the penalty is provided under Section 29(3) of the BIS Act, 2016 [1].

**Details**:
- Any person who contravenes the provisions of section 17 shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend up to two years [1].
- Alternatively, or in addition, they are punishable with a fine which shall not be less than two lakh rupees for the first contravention and not be less than five lakh rupees for the second and subsequent contraventions, but may extend up to ten times the value of goods or articles produced or sold or offered to be sold or affixed or applied with a Standard Mark, including Hallmark [1].
- Where the value of the goods or articles cannot be determined, it shall be presumed that one year's production was in such contravention, and the annual turnover in the previous financial year shall be taken as the value of the goods or articles [1].
- The offence under sub-section (3) is cognizable [1].

**Sources:**
[1] Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016: CHAPTER V > Section 29(3) Penalty for contravention, p. 14, dated 2016-03-22 | https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/BIS-Act-2016.pdf#page=14

## 6. हॉलमार्किंग क्या है और HUID कैसे चेक करें?

*groq:qwen/qwen3.8-27b · 7.5s · 3 citation(s) · check: 0 removed, 0 re-cited*

**Short answer**: हॉलमार्किंग कीमती धातुओं (सोना/चांदी) की शुद्धता की आधिकारिक जांच और रिकॉर्डिंग है [1]। HUID को BIS CARE ऐप के 'Verify HUID' फीचर से चेक किया जा सकता है [2][3]।

**Details**:
*   **हॉलमार्किंग क्या है**: यह कीमती धातुओं के आर्टिकल्स में मौजूद धातु के अनुपात की सटीक निर्धारण और आधिकारिक रिकॉर्डिंग है [1]। इसका मुख्य उद्देश्य उपभोक्ताओं को अशुद्धता से बचाना और निर्माताओं को कानूनी मानकों का पालन करने के लिए बाध्य करना है [1]। भारत में वर्तमान में सोना और चांदी को हॉलमार्किंग के दायरे में लाया गया है [1]।
*   **HUID क्या है**: HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) एक 6-अंकों का अल्फान्यूमेरिक नंबर है जो हर हॉलमार्क किए गए आइटम के लिए यूनिक और ट्रेसेबल होता है [3]। 1 जुलाई 2021 से हॉलमार्क में BIS लोगो, शुद्धता (कैरेटेज और फिनेस), और HUID नंबर शामिल होते हैं [3]।

**Step-by-step (HUID कैसे चेक करें)**:
1.  BIS CARE ऐप डाउनलोड करें [2][3]।
2.  ऐप में 'Verify HUID' फीचर का उपयोग करें [2][3]।
3.  हॉलमार्क किए गए आभूषण पर मौजूद HUID नंबर दर्ज करें [2][3]।
4.  ऐप से जुएलर के रजिस्ट्रेशन नंबर और संबंधित जानकारी प्राप्त करें [2]।
