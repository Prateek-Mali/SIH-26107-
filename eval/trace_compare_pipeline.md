## Full answers

### Run 1

**What went wrong / The situation:**
Your BIS licence application was found incomplete or did not conform to the required regulations, causing the Bureau to raise objections [1].

**How to fix it and deadlines:**
1. You must remove the indicated objections and submit your response within **thirty days** from the date you received the relevant communication from the Bureau [1].
2. Before taking any action to reject your application, the Bureau will give you a reasonable opportunity to be heard, either in person or through an authorised representative [1].

**What happens if you miss them:**
If your application remains incomplete or does not conform to the requirements after the given time and opportunities, your application **shall be rejected** by the Bureau [1].

Next step: You can manage your application and check status details on the Manak Online portal at https://www.manakonline.in/.

**Sources:**
[1] BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018: p. 2, dated 2018 | https://www.bis.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/BIS_CA_12032019.pdf#page=2
