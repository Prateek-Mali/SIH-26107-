## 1. What is BIS?

BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) is the National Standard Body of India established under the BIS Act 2016 for the harmonious development of the activities of standardization, marking, and quality certification of goods and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto [1]. It provides traceability and tangibility benefits to the national economy by providing safe reliable quality goods, minimizing health hazards to consumers, promoting exports and import substitutes, and controlling the proliferation of varieties through standardization, certification, and testing [1].

## 2. What does it do for manufacturers?
_rewritten: What does BIS do for manufacturers? | reused: [] | lang: en | refused: False (None) | 56.9 s_

For manufacturers, BIS provides a structured framework for quality certification and standardization of goods [1]. Through its certification schemes, BIS helps manufacturers establish that their products conform to relevant Indian Standards [2].

Here is what BIS does for manufacturers:
*   **Standardization and Certification:** BIS grants licences that allow manufacturers to use the BIS Standard Mark (ISI mark) on their products, which serves as a mark of quality and conformity to standards [2].
*   **Conformity Assessment:** BIS verifies that a product meets the required standards through testing, which can be conducted in third-party laboratories or within the manufacturer's own premises [2].
*   **Regulatory Compliance:** For certain products notified under Quality Control Orders (QCOs—government orders that make BIS certification compulsory), BIS provides the necessary certification to ensure legal compliance for manufacturing or importing [3].
*   **Operational Support:** BIS provides guidelines for managing licences, including procedures for changing the scope of a licence (adding or deleting product varieties) or updating manufacturing unit details [2].

## 4. Is it compulsory?
_rewritten: Is BIS certification compulsory for manufacturing stainless-steel water bottles under IS 17803: 2022? | reused: [] | lang: en | refused: False (None) | 10.4 s_

**YES** [1].

For your stainless-steel water bottles under IS 17803: 2022, BIS certification is compulsory under Scheme-I (ISI mark) [1][2]. The Bureau of Indian Standards acts as the certifying and enforcing authority under the Potable Water Bottles (Quality Control) Order [3]. Anyone who contravenes the provisions of this Quality Control Order (a government order that makes BIS certification compulsory) is punishable under the provisions of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 [3].

## 5. What documents do I need?
_rewritten: What documents are required for BIS ISI mark certification of stainless-steel water bottles under IS 17803: 2022? | reused: [] | lang: en | refused: False (None) | 59.7 s_

To obtain a BIS ISI mark licence for your stainless-steel water bottles under **IS 17803: 2022**, you must apply through the official BIS portal [1].

### Documents and Requirements for Application

*   **Supporting Documents:** You are required to submit relevant documents as specified in Form-V [2].
*   **Self-Evaluation:** You must submit a self-evaluation cum verification report as per the prescribed Annexure-I [2].
*   **Testing:** As part of the certification process, BIS will verify your production process and arrange for the drawing of samples from your factory for testing in a BIS-recognised laboratory [3]. The licence is granted only after the product demonstrates conformity to the standard through these test reports [3].

### Key Steps
1. **Prepare:** Ensure your manufacturing unit is ready for a factory inspection and that your product meets the requirements of IS 17803: 2022 [1].
2. **Testing:** Samples will be drawn and sent to a BIS-recognised laboratory for testing [3].
3. **Grant of Licence:** Once conformity is established and all requirements are met, the licence to use the Standard Mark (ISI mark) will be granted [3].

Here's what you need to do to complete the self-evaluation cum verification report as per Annexure-I for your stainless-steel water bottles under Scheme-I [1][2]:

1. **General Information**: Provide the applicant’s name, enclose your plant layout, and list any other premises owned or sub-contracted by you where part of the manufacturing activity is done, as applicable [2].
2. **Raw Materials**: Fill out the table for raw materials used by entering the serial number, name of the raw material, whether it has a BIS certification mark, the test certificate of the supplier, and how received batches or lots and the nature of the package are handled [2].
3. **Packing and Marking**: Detail the nature of packing, quantity per package, marking on the article, method of marking (such as printing, stencilling, or embossing), the form of label(s) if any (enclose one set), and batch or code numbering for identification [2]. You must also state in what manner the marking differs from the provisions in the Indian Standard Specification [2].
4. Submit this self-evaluation cum verification report as per Annexure-I along with your application in Form-V on the BIS website [1].
