## 1. what is ibs
_rewritten: None | refused=False (None) | 18.7 s_

BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) is the national standards body of India, operating under the BIS Act, 2016 [1][2]. It manages various certification schemes, such as product certification licences for manufacturers to use the Standard Mark on goods that conform to a standard, and hallmarking for precious metals [2]. While BIS certification is generally voluntary, it becomes mandatory for certain products notified by the Government of India under Quality Control Orders [3].

## 3. i make steel water bottles, which standard
_rewritten: Which BIS standard applies to steel water bottles? | refused=False (None) | 27.2 s_

The applicable standard for your product depends on its specific type:

| IS Number | Title | When it applies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| IS 17803: 2022 | Potable Water Bottles (Copper, Stainless Steel, Aluminum) | For non-insulated potable water bottles [1][2]. |
| IS 17526: 2021 | Domestic Stainless steel vacuum flask/bottle | For insulated vacuum flasks or bottles for domestic use [3]. |

Both products are under compulsory certification (Scheme-I/ISI mark) [1][3]. To obtain a licence, you must apply online via the Manak Online portal (https://www.manakonline.in/) using Form-V, along with the required documents and a self-evaluation cum verification report [4]. BIS will then conduct a factory inspection and product testing to assess your manufacturing infrastructure and quality control [5].

## 6. how do i apply — steps only
_rewritten: What are the steps to apply for a BIS licence for steel water bottles under Scheme I? | refused=False (None) | 33.5 s_

For your steel water bottles under IS 17803: 2022, you must apply under Scheme-I (ISI mark) [1]. The steps for the grant of a licence are:

1. Submit an application for the grant of a licence through the Manak Online portal [2].
2. Have all tests possible to be done in a day carried out at your facility [2].
3. Have a sample drawn for independent testing [2].
4. The grant of the licence shall not be withheld for the receipt of the independent test report [2].
5. An appropriate decision regarding the grant of the licence will be taken after the test report is received [2].

## 7. explain step 3
_rewritten: What is the process for having a sample drawn for independent testing as mentioned in step 3 of the BIS licence application for steel water bottles under IS 17803:2022? | refused=False (None) | 24.0 s_

*   **Sample Selection:** While drawing the sample, all related accessories, loads, or attachments must also be drawn to enable the laboratory to conduct complete testing of the product [1].
*   **Identification:** A unique QR code is generated by the system for the sample, and you are not required to write any physical code on it [1]. If the QR code cannot be scanned in the app, its unique number must be filled in the application [1].
*   **Verification:** The Bureau arranges a factory visit to verify the production process and draw the verification sample for testing at a third-party laboratory [2].
*   **Rejection Criteria:** Your application may be processed for rejection if samples are not offered for testing within 30 days of the application being recorded, or if the sample fails independent testing for the second time [3].
