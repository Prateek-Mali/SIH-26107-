## A1: What is IBS?
_23.6 s, refused=False, reason=None_

BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) has been established by the BIS Act, 2016 for the harmonious development of activities of standardization, quality certification, and marking of goods, and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto [1]. It grants licences to use a specified Standard Mark in relation to any goods or article which conforms to a standard [2]. BIS certification schemes are generally voluntary in nature, but can be made mandatory for certain products notified by the Government of India under Quality Control Orders [3].

## A5: What is an ISI mark?
_7.9 s, refused=False, reason=None_

The ISI mark is a Standard Mark established by the Bureau of Indian Standards to represent that goods conform to a particular Indian Standard [1]. It is used on articles, goods, their coverings, or attached labels to show conformity [1]. Products under Scheme I can display this mark once licensed, and licensees may advertise their products with the Standard Mark during the validity of their licence [2].

## A7: What is HUID?
_9.3 s, refused=False, reason=None_

HUID stands for Hallmark Unique Identification number, which is a six-digit alphanumeric number that is unique to each hallmarked item and fully traceable [1]. It serves as a key component of the hallmark on gold jewellery, which since 1st July 2021 also consists of the BIS logo and the purity of the article in caratage and fineness [1]. You can easily check the authenticity of a hallmarked item and view details such as the jeweller's registration number by using the 'Verify HUID' feature on the BIS Care App [1][2].

## A8: What is the difference between BIS and ISI?
_8.7 s, refused=False, reason=None_

The Indian Standards Institution (ISI) was established on 6 January 1947 as a Registered Society under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, with the mandate of preparing and promoting standards for adoption by Indian industry [1]. On the other hand, the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is established as a body corporate under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016, serving as the National Standards Body of India [2].
